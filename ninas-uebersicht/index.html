Ninas Uebersicht – Privacy Policy

Private single-user tool · last updated 18 October 2026

What this app is

Ninas Uebersicht is a private tool used by one person only: the owner of one Instagram professional account. It shows that person a short list of Instagram messages and comments that need her reply, so she does not have to open the Instagram app to find them. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it reads

  • Direct messages and comments of the account owner's own Instagram account (permissions instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_messages, instagram_business_manage_comments).
  • From these it keeps only the sender's username, a short excerpt of the text and the time, and only while the item still needs a reply.
  • It does not read or store likes, follower counts, statistics or profile data of other people.

What it never does

  • It never sends, edits or deletes messages or comments on its own. Every reply is written and sent by the account owner.
  • It never sells, shares or uses data for advertising, profiling or training of AI models.
  • It never accesses the data of any other Instagram account.

Where data is stored

The access token and the short list are stored only on the owner's own private home server, not with any third party. The list is removed as soon as an item is answered or becomes older than 14 days.

Deleting data and revoking access

The account owner can revoke access at any time under Instagram settings, Apps and Websites. After that the app can read nothing, and all stored data is deleted on request. To request deletion, write to the contact below; it is carried out within 7 days.
