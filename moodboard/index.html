What this app is

A private, single-user, read-only tool. It is used only by the owner of the Pinterest account “desete” to view his own boards and pins as a design moodboard inside his personal AI assistant.

Privacy Policy

  • Accesses only the owner's own boards, pins and basic account info (boards:read, pins:read, boards:read_secret, pins:read_secret, user_accounts:read).
  • Never accesses data of other Pinterest users.
  • Never creates, edits or deletes pins or boards.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings; no copies are kept elsewhere.
