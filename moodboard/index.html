Kupferweg Moodboard

Privacy Policy · Kupferweg (kupferweg.ch) · last updated 16 November 2026

What this app is

Kupferweg Moodboard is a private, single-user tool operated by Kupferweg. It connects the owner's own Pinterest account to his personal AI assistant (via the Model Context Protocol, MCP). It lets the assistant read the owner's own boards and pins to use them as a design moodboard (colours, styles, references), and lets the owner create or schedule pins on his own account for testing. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

  • Only the authenticated owner's own account data: boards, pins (public and secret) and basic profile information.
  • Scopes: boards:read, boards:read_secret, pins:read, pins:read_secret, user_accounts:read, and – only for the owner's own account – pins:write, boards:write.
  • No data of other Pinterest users is accessed.
  • No advertising, catalog or billing data is used.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner his own boards and pins inside his personal assistant.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.
  • No copies are kept on external servers; cached data can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under “Apps”. Revoking access stops all data access immediately; locally cached data is then deleted.
